Shiftwright shift texts

  • Program: Shiftwright shift texts. Messages about your work shifts, sent on behalf of your employer.
  • What you'll get: shift offers when a coworker calls out, confirmations when you pick up a shift, and notices when your schedule changes. You can text the number to tell your manager you can't make a shift.
  • How you sign up: by checking the consent box at shiftwright-production.up.railway.app/sms.html. Consent is not a condition of employment or of any purchase.
  • Message frequency varies, usually only when a shift needs covering or your schedule changes.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any message. You'll get one confirmation and no more messages. To start again, reply START.
  • For help: reply HELP, or contact us (below).
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Using Shiftwright

Shiftwright is an early-stage project, provided as is. Managers are responsible for the information they enter and for the schedules they publish. Shiftwright checks schedules against the labor rules in its dataset, but it does not give legal advice; employers remain responsible for following the law.

Don't misuse the service: no texting people who haven't agreed to it, no unlawful content, and no attempts to access other businesses' data.
