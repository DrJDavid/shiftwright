Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Shiftwright is operated by David Johnson, a sole proprietor.

What Shiftwright is

Shiftwright is a scheduling tool for businesses with hourly staff. Managers use it to build weekly schedules and to fill shifts when someone calls out. Employees may get text messages about their shifts.

What we collect

  • Managers: name, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and the business information they enter: locations, hours, roles, staffing needs and scheduling rules.
  • Employees (entered by their manager): name, age, pay rate, the roles they're trained for, availability, and mobile number.
  • Text messages: your mobile number, the messages we send you, your replies, and when you opted in or out, including the consent wording you agreed to.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: building schedules, sending shift offers and schedule notices, reading your replies (for example YES, NO, or "I can't make my shift"), and showing your manager the result.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with providers that run the service for us:

  • Twilio, to send and receive text messages;
  • Railway, which hosts the application and its database;
  • an AI model provider (through OpenRouter), which processes chat messages and text replies so the assistant can understand them.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the providers above as needed to deliver the messages.

Your employer can see the messages you exchange through Shiftwright about their shifts.

How long we keep it

As long as your employer's account is active, or until deletion is requested. Opt-out records are kept so we never text a number that said STOP.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop messages, or HELP for help.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, contact us (below) or ask your employer.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what the service needs.

Children

Shiftwright is for employers and their employees. Employers may enter information about employees under 18 as needed to follow minor-labor rules. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
