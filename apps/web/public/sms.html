Get shift texts from your employer

Shiftwright helps managers schedule hourly staff. If your employer uses Shiftwright, you can choose to get text messages about your shifts.

What you'll get: shift offers when a coworker calls out ("Can you cover Tue 4–10 pm? Reply YES or NO"), confirmations when you pick up a shift, and notices when your schedule changes. You can also text the number to tell your manager you can't make a shift.

The box is never pre-checked. Your number is used only for these messages and is never sold or shared for marketing.

Stopping texts

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. Reply HELP for help.
